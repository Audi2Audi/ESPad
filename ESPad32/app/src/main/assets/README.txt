Place your compiled espad_default_firmware.bin here before building the APK.

This is the firmware "Flash Default" flashes when tapped from the OTA
screen or Settings > OTA tab. Compile ESPad_PinConfig_Test.ino in
Arduino IDE, then use Sketch > Export Compiled Binary to produce the
.bin file, rename it to espad_default_firmware.bin, and place it here.
